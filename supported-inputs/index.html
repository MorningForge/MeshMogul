Compatibility

Check your build before converting.

MeshMogul works with supported source and readable content. An unsupported piece blocks the selected root; it is not quietly omitted.

Current release: MeshMogul 1.3.0. Start with compatibility: Supported solid Union/CSG and transparent Glass Union workflows are now included. Not every Union, mixed assembly, or Glass appearance state is compatible. Child Decal/Texture overlays and unreadable mesh content remain outside the workflow. No universal model conversion or performance improvement is promised.

Two-sided MeshPart appearance.

MeshMogul retains supported DoubleSided MeshPart appearance through Preview and reuse. Geometry must be readable and appearance must be supported. Check both faces of your retained Preview and inserted result under your intended lighting; no universal texture, material, or permission guarantee applies.

Appearance: transfer has boundaries.

Supported appearance grouping lets multiple compatible source states produce ordered mesh outputs within one grouped Preview and one logical Assets record. Grouped outputs predate 1.3 and remain supported, with appearance preservation still conditional on the supported inputs below.

Supported source colors are carried in mesh data. Compatible material, transparency, and reflectance states can be partitioned into separate outputs. Readable UV and image channels are required for supported textured cases.

  • Glass support is bounded. Compatible transparent Glass Union workflows are supported, including mixed Glass and opaque outputs. This is not blanket support for every Glass source, transparency state, or rendering effect; inspect the retained Preview and reusable result.
  • Child Decal and Texture overlays are refused. They are not flattened into a baked image.
  • Texture and SurfaceAppearance support is bounded. Readable, durable image resources and supported channel combinations are required. Arbitrary PBR fidelity is not guaranteed.
  • MaterialVariant is conditional. Supported mapless definitions require compatible definitions at the destination; arbitrary mapped variants are not supported.
  • Copied properties do not prove identical pixels. Inspect Preview and inserted results in your lighting and destination place.

Size, selection, and source.

Conversion checks a limit of 60,000 vertices and 20,000 triangles. There is no general automatic simplification or chunking promise. Multiple independently selected roots are refused; put supported geometry under one root when appropriate.

A deliberate option can exclude fully invisible supported parts. This does not allow unsupported descendants to bypass preflight. Original source is retained; scripts, constraints, source physics, and animation are not copied into the visual derivative.

Four different performance questions.

In 1.3, bounded hierarchy traversal and incremental, frame-sliced inspection reduce the risk of expensive Studio stalls. Pathological selections can be refused; this is not a guarantee that every enormous Workspace is safe to inspect or convert.

  • Editor responsiveness: how Studio responds while MeshMogul inspects the selection.
  • Conversion time: how long geometry capture and Preview processing take.
  • Mesh complexity: output count, geometry, textures, and collision data.
  • Runtime performance: measured rendering, physics, memory, and script cost in the actual experience.

Improving inspection does not establish a universal gain in the other three. Preview progress and cancellation help you follow or stop work; they do not remove platform limits.

Publication and reuse.

  • Publishing currently requires a manual Studio Command Bar handoff and Roblox permission to create the requested assets.
  • A grouped logical asset can require several physical mesh uploads. An incomplete result cannot Insert.
  • No automatic same-AssetId updates, rollback, placed-copy propagation, or one-click Creator Store publishing.
  • Assets records are local plugin settings: 25 recent complete records and up to 100 pinned records. Unresolved incomplete records are protected from normal complete-record eviction.
  • No team/cloud sync or guaranteed triangle-count, draw-call, or FPS improvement. Compare appearance, collision, and performance before replacing a build.
