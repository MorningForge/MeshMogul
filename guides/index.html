Something not working?

Start with what you can see. Follow the explanation to a practical check.

More symptoms and fixes
  • Wheels or doors fused into chassis
  • Pivot rotates around the wrong origin
  • Wrong stud scale or orientation after import
  • Materials fused onto one surface
  • Generated ProceduralModel disappears or regenerates unexpectedly
  • ForceGeneration or GenerationError failure
  • Nested ProceduralModel refused by MeshMogul
  • Baked mesh rotates around bounding center instead of hinge
  • Invisible collider blocks got excluded or included unexpectedly
  • Lights or attachments missing after bake
  • Should I Group, Union, or publish a MeshPart?
  • Union destroyed my textures or smudged shading
  • Can I ship meshes without Blender?
  • When do I actually need Blender?
  • Pasted Creator Store ID into MeshId and got an error
  • MeshPart shows empty MeshId in Preview
  • Other place fails to load private mesh asset
  • Diagonal shadow smudge on flat box faces
  • Normal map bumps look inverted (DirectX vs OpenGL Y+)
  • Blurry textures or mobile VRAM crashes
  • Expected 1 MeshPart but got a Model of 3 MeshParts
  • Multi-color stripes merged, Wood vs Metal split apart
  • Mapless MaterialVariant warning after insert
  • Scripts or hinges missing after converting a Model
  • Exact-instance Find vs global search confusion
  • Local Assets library vs Roblox Packages
  • MeshId changed after generating a texture
  • Texture looks blurry or stretched on bevels
  • SurfaceAppearance overrides TextureID
  • Properties window shows no MeshId on Model or Part
  • MeshId field is empty on Preview MeshPart
  • Partial multi-group upload failure
  • Group permission error after publishing
  • Accidental duplicate uploads from rerunning Command Bar
  • EditableMesh vertex or triangle limit exceeded
  • Stretched Cylinder or Ball came out circular/spherical
  • Mapped MaterialVariant or Decal refused in preflight
  • Published mesh disappears at distance (RenderFidelity LOD)
  • Backfaces missing from underneath
  • Unsure whether mesh conversion actually helped performance
  • Merging entire street made framerate worse
  • MicroProfiler shows Blue or Red render bars
  • Stacked blocks waste triangles internally
  • Invisible wall blocking an open arch or doorway
  • High PhysicsParts memory even with CanCollide false
  • Cannot assign CollisionFidelity from runtime script
