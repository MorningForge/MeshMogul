Founding · 1.1.1

One asset can have more than one mesh.

What’s new in 1.1.1 →
01 / PREVIEW

Keep supported groups together

Compatible appearance groups can become separate ordered outputs within one logical Preview. Inspect the result before publishing.

02 / APPEARANCE

Preserve the distinctions that fit

Preserve supported material, transparency, color and surface distinctions. Inspect Preview; arbitrary texture baking and universal PBR fidelity are not included.

03 / REUSE

Remember the whole result

Grouped Assets records keep ordered output mesh IDs. Insert a complete result, then use exact-instance Find to locate the tracked inserted root in the current place.

More control in 1.1.1

Inspect. Confirm. Keep moving.

Retain a Preview, inspect it from the right angle, and use Reset Position to restore its approved position and angle. Copy ordered source MeshId references without confusing them with newly published outputs.

Publication follows the current source.

Freshness checks guard against stale source. Partial results keep confirmed IDs while incomplete grouped records remain under Needs attention and cannot Insert.
